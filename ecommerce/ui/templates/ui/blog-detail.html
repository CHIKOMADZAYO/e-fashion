A simpler way to build your everyday wardrobe

The clothes we wear most are often the ones that make getting dressed feel easy. A good wardrobe doesn’t need to be complicated; it needs a few pieces that work well together and feel right when you put them on.

Start with what you already love

Notice the things you reach for on busy mornings. Maybe it’s a shirt that always sits just right, a pair of trousers that goes with everything, or a soft layer you keep by the door. Those are your starting points.

Make room for small discoveries

Build around those familiar favourites, then add something with a different texture or a fresh colour. The best outfits usually have a little balance: one piece you know well and one that makes it feel new.

Style is less about having more choices and more about finding the pieces that feel like you.

Keep the pieces that make your everyday easier. Let the rest be an invitation to experiment.
